Terms marked **(P1)** are already used in the Phase 1 screens (menu, sign-in, account, dashboard); the rest are the agreed choices for the later phases, so they are written once and not re-decided per screen.

## Choices worth a second opinion

- **Urdu** keeps the words Pakistani business users already say in English (بکنگ، واؤچر، رسید، پلاٹ، بجٹ، کمیشن) and uses Urdu for the ones with a real everyday word (قسط، معاہدہ، ٹھیکیدار، چھٹی، بیعانہ). "Voucher" is spelled واؤچر in Urdu and واوچر in Pashto, as each language's usual spelling; make them match if reviewers prefer.
- **Arabic** uses Modern Standard Arabic accounting terms (سند, قيد يومية, تسوية بنكية). A Gulf or Egyptian accountant may prefer another word for "voucher" (سند قبض/صرف) once the voucher types are translated in Phase 3.
- **French** translates "voucher" as *pièce comptable* (accounting document) rather than *bon*, because here it is a ledger document, not a coupon.
- **German** uses *Beleg* for voucher and *Rate* for installment; *Quittung* only for a receipt.
- **Japanese** uses 伝票 for voucher and 領収書 for receipt; "marla/kanal" are kept in katakana plus the original word because there is no Japanese equivalent.
- **Chinese** has no standard word for marla/kanal: the original word stays in brackets. 回款 (money coming back in) is used for collections, 应收款 for receivables.
